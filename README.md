<h1>Bow Expansion</h1> 
<h3>Overview</h3>
This is a Fabric <b>only</b> mod. It's a very simple mod that adds a few extra bows to the game.
<br> This mod does not include custom arrows or enchantments of any kind.
<br><br>
The custom bows provided by this mod are: <br><br>

- Copper bow
- Golden bow (same damage as copper, but lower durability and shorter draw time)
- Iron bow
- Emerald bow
- Obsidian bow
- Diamond bow
- Netherite bow
<br>

> [!Note]
> Adding infinity to these bows works with modded arrows as well... at least it should
